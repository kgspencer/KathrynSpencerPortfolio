I'm Kathryn, a recent graduate and creative programmer.

I'm passionate about design and development that feels human. I have a background in technical programming as well as a long-lasting appreciation for visual design and storytelling. Fostering these two passions led me to pursue front-end development and design, where I now seek to turn my passion into professional experience.
