I'm Kathryn, a recent graduate and creative technologist.

I'm passionate about design and development that feels human. I have a background in technical programming as well as a long-lasting appreciation for visual design and storytelling. I'm fascinated by how the act of documenting something changes it (see below).

In my work I explore how different mediums and technologies afford different epistemological experiences. I aim to explore this professionally through archival work.

I hold a bachelor's degree in computer science from Mount Holyoke College.
